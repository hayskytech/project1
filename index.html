Our college

A tradition of education and service

St. Joseph's Degree College is governed by St. Joseph's Educational Society, registered under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860 (Society No. 19 of 1967). The college has been serving the cause of education with sincere efforts, preparing students for life while imbibing the values of Indian culture and tradition. It is a self-financed co-education college without grant-in-aid.

Learning shaped by education, culture, and tradition.
